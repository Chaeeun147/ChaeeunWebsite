Sound bomb
This is a "Sound Effect Bomb" me and two of my friends created at a hackclub hackathon, Stasis in Austin, Texas! The idea of the project is that it is a C4-inspired sound effect player. When you are in an awkward situation that requires a sound effect to fill the void, or when you wish you could just play a meme sound effect, this device would be perfect for you!!!!
