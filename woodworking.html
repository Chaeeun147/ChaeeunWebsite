My Woodworking Projects

Key Cabinets
This is a key cabniet I created! It was one shelf, five hooks in the inside of the cabinet, and three hooks on the outisde bottom. Whenever I travel, I always buy keychains, and I wanted a place where I can store and display them, so, I decided to make this cabinet.

Coffee Table
This is a coffee table I made for my as an assignment in woodworking class! It was a fun project. Now, I use it as a table to put things like drinks, snacks, laptops, etc. beside my couch.

Campfire Hamilton Sign
This is a the sign I made for Campfire Hamilton, an event I co-organized! I made the base of the sign with wood and lazer printed the logo of the event! It was a great touch to the event, and we got all the attendees to sign the sign as a memnto!
